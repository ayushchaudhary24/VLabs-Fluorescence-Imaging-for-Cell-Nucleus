Precautions

  1. Turn off or dim the room lights during the fluorescence experiment.
  2. Minimize the fluorophore-treated specimen’s exposure to excitation light to reduce photobleaching and loss of fluorescence signal.
  3. Always use the appropriate UV-protective shield while viewing through the eyepiece. Never look directly into the excitation beam.
